Nobody here is waiting for you

Elsewhere County runs on its own clock. Citizens wake, work, eat, worship, argue, fall in and out of love, spend money, keep secrets and go to bed. You are a visitor with no quest to give them.

Relationships
Citizens build affinity on their own. Affinity shifts through chats, arguments, sermons, shared meals and town events.

Assumption: the county begins on a Monday in late spring. A full day takes about six real minutes at 1×. Nothing you do is required for the town to continue.
