How to Investigate

Move

W A S D walk, Shift run, mouse to look. Click the scene to capture the cursor. Esc releases it.

Work the scene

Approach the glinting markers and press E. Every piece of evidence is logged to your casebook. Some clues only resolve in the lab.

Casebook

Tab opens the case file: evidence, suspects, map, the sect, notes. You can fast-travel to known locations from the map.

Interviews

Press E on a person. Ask about the victim, the sect, alibis. Present evidence to break a story. People remember how you treat them.

Warrant

Background checks reveal physical traits. Compare them against the lab results, verify alibis, then request a warrant on the Suspects page.

Stay human

Sleep, eat, and keep your stress down. The town does not pause for you, and the killer is watching how you work.
