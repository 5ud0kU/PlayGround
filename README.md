## GENESIS

GENESIS is a procedurally generated microbial-world game. Each run builds a new environment and population, then explores how inherited variation and environmental pressures shape the organisms over time.

The biology is intentionally a **model, not a laboratory simulator**. Concepts such as DNA inheritance, mutation, gene expression, cell division, and natural selection are grounded in real biology, but are simplified into game rules and do not predict real organisms. The generated landscape, resource fields, visual cell structures, and time scale are presentation and gameplay abstractions. Cellular-part archive entries are educational model proxies—not real organelles, treatments, or transferable biological material.
